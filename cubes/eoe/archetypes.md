# EOE Set Cube — Archetype Discovery Report

**Mode:** Guided Coverage — user rule: *every rare and mythic must be linked to at least one
archetype if its oracle text honestly supports one; genuine misses are reported, not forced.*
Parsed filter: `rarity in {rare, mythic}` → **80 cards** in scope (see Coverage Appendix).

**Tag coverage going in:** 271/271 mainboard cards tagged (0 untagged).

**Result:** 16 archetypes (7 Core, 5 Supported, 4 Build-Around). 77/80 rares & mythics placed;
3 honestly unplaceable (reasons in the appendix).

### Artifacts & Spacecraft — Core
**Colors:** All five; U/R/W lean  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Sami, Wildcat Captain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sami,+Wildcat+Captain"), [Tezzeret, Cruel Captain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Tezzeret,+Cruel+Captain"), [Mm'menon, the Right Hand](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mm'menon,+the+Right+Hand")
**Core cards:** [Emissary Escort](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Emissary+Escort"), [Warmaker Gunship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Warmaker+Gunship"), [Weapons Manufacturing](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Weapons+Manufacturing"), [Mechan Assembler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mechan+Assembler"), [Oreplate Pangolin](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Oreplate+Pangolin"), [Uthros, Titanic Godcore](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Uthros,+Titanic+Godcore") (+100 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Artifacts, Tokens, Counters (+1/+1)
**Why it works:** 111 of 271 cards carry the Artifacts tag — this is the cube's backbone. Count
payoffs scale with raw artifact density ("this creature gets +X/+0, where X is the greatest mana
value among other artifacts" — Emissary Escort; "deals damage equal to the number of artifacts you
control" — Warmaker Gunship), while Sami gives every spell affinity and Mm'menon casts artifacts
off the library. [Hardlight Containment](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hardlight+Containment") ("Enchant artifact you control") and [The Seriema](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"The+Seriema") (tutors any legendary creature) slot in cross-tag.

**Keystones:** [Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dawnsire,+Sunstar+Dreadnought"), [Sunstar Chaplain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sunstar+Chaplain"), [Sami, Ship's Engineer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sami,+Ship's+Engineer")

**Why it works:** Discovered via the keyword scan — no tag covers it. Six payoffs read "if you
control two or more tapped creatures" (Chaplain, Vanguard, Coordinator, War-Rager, Sami, Trooper)
and seven engines trigger "whenever this creature becomes tapped" (Navigator, Pilot, [Chrome Companion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chrome+Companion"), [Seedship Agrarian](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Seedship+Agrarian"), [Nanoform Sentinel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nanoform+Sentinel"), [Hemosymbic Mite](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hemosymbic+Mite"), [Swarm Culler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Swarm+Culler")). into fuel for both. Cross-tag: [Sunset Saboteur](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sunset+Saboteur") — 4 power on a 2-mana body is premium station
fuel; [Tapestry Warden](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Tapestry+Warden") lets high-toughness creatures "station permanents using its toughness";
[Loading Zone](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Loading+Zone") doubles charge counters; [Zealous Display](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zealous+Display") pumps every stationer's power.

**Core cards:** [Virus Beetle](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Virus+Beetle"), [Temporal Intervention](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Temporal+Intervention"), [Alpharael, Stonechosen](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Alpharael,+Stonechosen"), [Sunset Saboteur](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sunset+Saboteur")
**Key themes:** Forced discard, Wheels, Station
**Why it works:** At one charge counter, "Whenever an opponent discards a card, they lose 3 life";
at 8+ it forces a discard on every attack itself. Support is honest but light: Virus Beetle and
Temporal Intervention force discards, and cross-tag, the ward costs on Alpharael, Stonechosen
("Ward—Discard a card at random") and Sunset Saboteur ("Ward—Discard a card") make opponents'
removal spells drain them for 3.
