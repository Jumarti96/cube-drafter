## Core

### Station & Tap Crew — Core
**Colors:** WR payoffs; Spacecraft in all five colors  **Macro lean:** Aggro/Midrange
**Keystones:** [Dawnsire, Sunstar Dreadnought](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dawnsire,+Sunstar+Dreadnought"), [Sunstar Chaplain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sunstar+Chaplain"), [Sami, Ship's Engineer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sami,+Ship's+Engineer")
**Core cards:** [Dawnstrike Vanguard](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dawnstrike+Vanguard"), [Flight-Deck Coordinator](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Flight-Deck+Coordinator"), [Frontline War-Rager](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Frontline+War-Rager"), [Vaultguard Trooper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vaultguard+Trooper"), [Mechan Navigator](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mechan+Navigator"), [Starfighter Pilot](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Starfighter+Pilot"), [Wedgelight Rammer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wedgelight+Rammer"), [Warmaker Gunship](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Warmaker+Gunship") (35 cards total)
**Key themes:** Station; tapped-creatures-matter; Artifacts
**Why it works:** Station taps your creatures as fuel, and the payoffs turn that cost into a
benefit. All six "two or more tapped creatures" payoffs are W or R (`Dawnstrike Vanguard`,
`Flight-Deck Coordinator`, `Sunstar Chaplain`, `Frontline War-Rager`, `Vaultguard Trooper`,
`Sami, Ship's Engineer`) — that is what makes this pair WR. The other seven tap payoffs, the
"whenever this creature becomes tapped" triggers, are scattered elsewhere (`Mechan Navigator` and
`Nanoform Sentinel` in U, `Swarm Culler` in B, `Seedship Agrarian` and `Hemosymbic Mite` in G),
and the 22 Spacecraft they load are spread W5 · C5 · U4 · B4 · R4 · G4. Any color can supply ships
and incidental tap value; only WR gets paid for tapping wide.

each creature you control," and it stations into an 8/8 flying trampler.

### Sacrifice, Void & Aristocrats — Core
**Colors:** BR (W via Syr Vondam)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Sothera, the Supervoid](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sothera,+the+Supervoid"), [Alpharael, Stonechosen](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Alpharael,+Stonechosen"), [Susur Secundi, Void Altar](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Susur+Secundi,+Void+Altar"), [Ragost, Deft Gastronaut](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Ragost,+Deft+Gastronaut")
**Core cards:** [Lightless Evangel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lightless+Evangel"), [Susurian Voidborn](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Susurian+Voidborn"), [Memorial Vault](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Memorial+Vault"), [Biotech Specialist](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Biotech+Specialist"), [Voidforged Titan](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Voidforged+Titan"), [Syr Vondam, Sunstar Exemplar](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Syr+Vondam,+Sunstar+Exemplar"), [Mutinous Massacre](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mutinous+Massacre"), [Plasma Bolt](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Plasma+Bolt") (44 cards total)
**Key themes:** Aristocrats/Sacrifice; Void; Artifacts; Tokens
**Why it works:** The 31-card Sacrifice cluster (B17 · R9) merges with the 14-card Void suite,
whose trigger reads "if a nonland permanent left the battlefield this turn." Every fodder death
switches on removal, drain and draw in the same turn. `Biotech Specialist` makes a Lander and then
reads "Whenever you sacrifice an artifact, this creature deals 2 damage to target opponent" — the
Lander sacrifices itself for a land, so the damage costs nothing.

### Warp → Void Control — Core
**Colors:** UB  **Macro lean:** Control/Tempo
**Keystones:** [Alpharael, Stonechosen](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Alpharael,+Stonechosen"), [Chorale of the Void](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chorale+of+the+Void"), [Starfield Vocalist](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Starfield+Vocalist"), [Singularity Rupture](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Singularity+Rupture")
**Core cards:** [Codecracker Hound](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Codecracker+Hound"), [Starbreach Whale](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Starbreach+Whale"), [Sinister Cryologist](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sinister+Cryologist"), [Quantum Riddler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Quantum+Riddler"), [Insatiable Skittermaw](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Insatiable+Skittermaw"), [Hymn of the Faller](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hymn+of+the+Faller"), [Voidforged Titan](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Voidforged+Titan"), [Temporal Intervention](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Temporal+Intervention"), [Unravel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Unravel"), [Zero Point Ballad](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zero+Point+Ballad") (27 cards total)
**Key themes:** Warp; Void; Self-Mill; permission
**Why it works:** The pair the previous revision missed entirely, and the link is a single clause.
Void triggers "if a nonland permanent left the battlefield this turn **or a spell was warped this
turn**." Every other Void deck has to lose a permanent to switch it on; a blue deck simply warps
something. Blue is the deepest warp color (`Codecracker Hound`, `Sinister Cryologist`,
`Starbreach Whale`, `Quantum Riddler`, `Mechanozoa`, `Starfield Vocalist`, `Starwinder`) and black
holds 11 of the 14 Void payoffs. UB also owns all three counterspells (`Annul`, `Unravel`,
`Divert Disaster`) and both graveyard-filling sweepers, so the shell has a real control back end
rather than only a synergy.

## Cube Substrate — mechanics, not archetypes

These are cube-wide and inform nearly every deck, which is why they are no longer archetype rows.
The previous revision listed "Artifacts & Spacecraft" as a Core archetype with 111 supporting cards
— 41% of the cube — which made it overlap almost every other entry while telling a drafter nothing
about what to move toward.

- **Artifacts — 109 tagged cards** (R30 · U24 · B21 · W19 · C18 · G12). Affinity and
  cast-from-anywhere effects (`Sami, Wildcat Captain`, `Mm'menon, the Right Hand`,
  `Tezzeret, Cruel Captain`) reward going deep, but artifact density is this cube's default state
  rather than a choice. Count payoffs like `Emissary Escort` and `Warmaker Gunship` scale in any
  deck.
- **Station / Spacecraft — 27 cards, 22 of them Spacecraft**, spread W5 · C5 · U4 · B4 · R4 · G4.
  Every color gets ships; only WR gets paid for the tapping, which is what Station & Tap Crew
  captures.
- **Warp — 32 cards**, W8 · U8 · R6 · G6 · B2. A tempo discount available to four colors, and the
  free enabler for Void.
- **Void — 14 cards**, B11 · R4 (plus `Interceptor Mechan` in BR). The one substrate mechanic that
  *is* color-bound, which is why it anchors two archetypes (BR and UB) rather than none.
- **Lander tokens — 15 sources**, G-heavy but present in every color. Fixing, ramp, landfall fuel
  and sacrifice fodder in a single token.

## Checked and deliberately not listed

Reported so each absence reads as a finding rather than an oversight:

- **Flyers-matter** — 33 creatures have flying (U12 deepest), but only 5 cards reference flying at
  all, and three of those (`Desculpting Blast`, `Shattered Wings`, `Skystinger`) are removal, not
  payoffs. No archetype.
- **Mill as a win condition** — 6 cards mill at all, and every one mills for fuel or as a rider.
  `Space-Time Anomaly` and `Singularity Rupture` are listed under the decks that use the graveyard,
  not as a deck-out plan.
- **Energy** — 0 cards.
- **Planeswalkers** — 1 (`Tezzeret, Cruel Captain`); no superfriends shell.
