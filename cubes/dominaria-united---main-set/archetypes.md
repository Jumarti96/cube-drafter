### Could not be placed (6)

**Adarkar Wastes, Caves of Koilos, Karplusan Forest, Shivan Reef, Sulfurous Springs, Yavimaya Coast** — the painland cycle. Each reads only "{T}: Add {C}" / "{T}: Add [two colors]. This land deals 1 damage to you." They have **no basic land types** (so they do not advance Domain, unlike Thran Portal), no legendary text (unlike Plaza of Heroes), and no synergy hook of any kind. They are premium generic fixing that improves *every* two-color deck in their pair equally — which is exactly why no single archetype in this cube can honestly claim them. Reported unplaced per the coverage rule rather than forced into a fabricated fit.
