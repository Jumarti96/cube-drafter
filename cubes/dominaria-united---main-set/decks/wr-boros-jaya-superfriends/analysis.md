## ANALYSIS

### DECK IDENTITY

A low-curve Boros token aggro deck that treats Superfriends as an accelerant rather than a plan. Jaya, Fiery Negotiator is the archetype's real engine here - '+1: Create a 1/1 red Monk creature token with prowess' adds a body every turn, and Urza Assembles the Titans chapter III turns that into two Monks per turn. Underneath sits a shell that makes two or three bodies per card (Resolute Reinforcements, Argivian Cavalier, kicked Keldon Strike Team) with Valiant Veteran as a turn-2 anthem over the Soldier tokens and Squee, Dubious Monarch adding a free hasty Goblin on every attack, and it converts that width to lethal with kicked Heroic Charge's trample. Average mana value is 2.61 with only five cards at MV 4 or above. Stated plainly: the deck is built to win by turn 7 WITHOUT drawing Urza - the Saga is upside on an already-lethal board, not a prerequisite, and both grill agents independently flagged that all three of its chapters key off a 2-card denominator.

### SUPERFRIENDS AS AN ACCELERANT, NOT A PLAN

This is the only one of the four builds where the planeswalkers are not the point. Both grill agents independently reached the same conclusion about the keystone, and the record states it rather than arguing with it:

> Urza Assembles the Titans has three chapters, and **all three key off the same 2-card denominator**. Chapter I ("If a planeswalker card is revealed this way, put it into your hand") hits 2 of 39; chapter II ("put a planeswalker card with mana value 6 or less from your hand onto the battlefield") needs one of those exact 2 cards already in hand; chapter III needs a walker already on the battlefield.

What makes that acceptable here is that the deck wins without it. The damage comes from an unconditional floor of 7 Soldier bodies, Squee's free Goblin on every attack, Jaya's Monk every turn, and kicked Heroic Charge's trample. Urza is upside on an already-lethal board.

The Phase 9 Challenger argued for cutting Urza entirely, and the argument was good. It was contested on one reproduced fact: **Urza Assembles the Titans is the only card in the entire 271-card pool tagged with the "Superfriends" synergy cluster.** Cutting it does not make a worse Superfriends deck; it makes a deck that is not one.

### THE 5TH RARE: A REAL DISAGREEMENT, RESOLVED TWICE

The fifth rare/mythic slot was deliberately left open for the three sketchers to choose between two cards, and they split 2–1:

| | Serra Redeemer `{3}{W}{W}` | Valiant Veteran `{1}{W}` |
|---|---|---|
| Effect | Two +1/+1 counters on every creature with power ≤2 that enters | Other Soldiers get +1/+1 |
| Catches Jaya's Monks? | **Yes** | **No** — Monks are not Soldiers |
| Catches Squee's Goblins? | **Yes** | **No** |
| Permanent? | Yes, counters | No, dies with its source |
| Mana value | **5** | **2** |

The judge upheld Valiant Veteran on curve, while explicitly recording that the losing side was right on the mechanics. Then the Challenger found that the framing had been a **false choice**: once Plaza of Heroes was cut (below), a rare slot was free and the two were never mutually exclusive.

That half was accepted. The slot still did not go to Serra Redeemer, because the objection to it was never scarcity — it was that MV 5 in a deck already over the Aggro curve ceiling attacks the exact property this build was selected for. It went to **Squee, Dubious Monarch** (`{2}{R}`, MV 3) instead: *"Haste / Whenever Squee attacks, create a 1/1 red Goblin creature token that's tapped and attacking / You may cast this card from your graveyard by paying {3}{R}..."* — the recurring-body count the Challenger wanted, at a cost the curve can pay, plus a second answer to the sweeper class.

### THE SAME CARD, THE OPPOSITE VERDICT

Plaza of Heroes is in the Mardu build and cut from this one, and the count is the whole reason.

Its useful text is *"{T}: Add one mana of any color. Spend this mana only to cast a **legendary** spell."* This deck's legendary cards are Jaya, Karn and Baird — 3 of 23. Karn costs `{4}` and needs no coloured mana at all, so the ability is colour-relevant for **2 of 23 nonland cards**. For the other 20 — including every double-pip cost in the deck (`{3}{W}{W}` Urza, `{2}{W}{W}` Heroic Charge, `{2}{R}{R}` Jaya) — Plaza's only available mode is *"{T}: Add {C}"*.

So it was one of seventeen lands producing no usable colour for 87% of the spells, **and** it was consuming one of five rare slots. In the three-colour Mardu deck the same card fixes three colours for the walker package and earns its slot easily.

### THE STATISTIC THAT WAS WRONG

The lens "lowest-curve / most explosive" was selected over two rival sketches, and the number cited in its favour was a 58% turn-1 deployment rate. That number was wrong, and the Challenger caught it.

The goldfish tool counts any MV-1 card as a turn-1 play. Two of this deck's four MV-1 cards were **Hammerhand**, whose type line is `Enchantment — Aura` and whose first line is `Enchant creature` — on an empty turn-1 board there is no legal object to enchant.

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| P(≥1 of 4 MV-1 cards in an opening 7 of 40) | 55.2% |
| **P(≥1 genuine turn-1 body — Phoenix Chick only)** | **32.3%** |

The record now states 32.3% and withdraws the "highest turn-1 rate of the four builds" claim, which was never computed on a comparable basis for the other three.

### A CURVE BAND THAT CANNOT BE MET

The structural gate raises two curve WARNs. The MV4+ one is ordinary — every card at MV 4 or above is either a locked rare that *is* the archetype (Urza, Jaya, Karn) or the named finisher (Heroic Charge ×2).

The MV-1 WARN is different: **the band is unmeetable with cards that exist.** Every MV ≤ 1 nonland card in the pool whose colour identity fits W/R:

| Card | Why it cannot fill the slot |
|---|---|
| Phoenix Chick | Already at its 2-copy cap |
| Clockwork Drawbridge | `Defender` — cannot attack |
| Walking Bulwark | `Defender` — cannot attack |
| Shivan Devastator | `{X}{R}`; a 0/0 at X=0, and mythic |
| Hammerhand | Aura, not a body |
| Flowstone Infusion | Instant |
| Inscribed Tablet, Vanquisher's Axe | Artifacts, not bodies |

Raising the MV-1 share is not available at any price in this pool.

### KICKERS THAT ARE ALWAYS ON

Unlike the other three builds, this deck has no card that is legal only through a *declined* kicker. Four cards print RW identities purely from kicker costs, and in W/R **both halves of every one are core colours**:

| Card | Kicker | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Keldon Strike Team | `{1}{W}` | Two extra Soldier tokens — one body becomes three |
| Hurloon Battle Hymn | `{W}` | 4 life on top of 4 damage |
| Heroic Charge | `{1}{R}` | Trample for the whole team |
| Cleaving Skyrider (SB) | `{2}{R}` | X damage to any target, X = attackers |

The honest consequence, which the Challenger raised and the record now carries: if the deck pays those kickers as intended, its *real* MV4+ share is 7 of 23 (30.4%) and its real average mana value is 2.87, not 2.61. Re-running `land_target` at that figure still returns 17 lands, so there is no mana consequence — but the curve WARN is understated by about 9pp on the deck's own stated play pattern.

### WHAT HURLOON BATTLE HYMN DOES NOT DO

*"Hurloon Battle Hymn deals 4 damage to **target creature or planeswalker**. If this spell was kicked, you gain 4 life."*

An earlier version of this analysis called it an "8-point swing in a race." The damage cannot go to the opponent's face. Against a creature-based aggressor it is genuinely a large swing — kill an attacker, gain 4 — but against a burn or evasive clock it gains 4 life and nothing else.

- Curve WARN on MV4+ share, accepted with a mechanism rather than repaired: 5 of 23 = 21.7% against an Aggro ceiling of 20% (30.4% under the deck's own kicker intent - see count_dependent_verdicts). The five cards are Jaya (4), Karn (4), Heroic Charge x2 (4) and Urza Assembles the Titans (5). Three are locked rare/mythic slots that ARE the Superfriends archetype this deck was commissioned to build, and the other two are the mass-pump finisher the thesis kill_mechanism names by function. There is no MV4+ card here that could be cut without cutting either the archetype or the win condition.

- Curve WARN on MV1 share (13% against a 15% floor), accepted because THE BAND CANNOT BE MET WITH CARDS THAT EXIST. I enumerated every MV<=1 nonland card in the 271-card pool whose colour identity fits W/R: Clockwork Drawbridge ({W}, Artifact Creature - Wall, Defender), Flowstone Infusion ({R}, Instant), Hammerhand ({R}, Enchantment - Aura), Inscribed Tablet ({1}, Artifact), Phoenix Chick ({R}, Creature - Phoenix), Shivan Devastator ({X}{R}, mythic, a 0/0 at X=0), Vanquisher's Axe ({1}, Equipment), Walking Bulwark ({1}, Artifact Creature - Golem, Defender). The ONLY MV-1 creature that can attack is Phoenix Chick, already at its 2-copy cap; the two other MV-1 creatures both have Defender, and Shivan Devastator would cost a rare slot to be a 0/0. Raising the MV1 share is not available at any price in this pool.

- Assembly PASS on both roles. payoff is 5 copies / 4.6 effective at p=0.82 (Jaya, Valiant Veteran, Squee, plus Heroic Charge x2 weighted 0.8 because 'Creatures you control get +2/+1' does nothing from an empty board and its trample clause needs Kicker {1}{R} on top of {2}{W}{W}). enabler is 9 copies / 8.6 effective at p=0.97 (Keldon Strike Team x2 weighted 0.8 because its two extra Soldier tokens require Kicker {1}{W}).

- Goldfish PASS at 85% keepable (floor 80%) and 3 lands by turn 3 at 88%. The tool's 'T1 48%' figure is NOT quoted as a deployment rate anywhere in this record - see the turn-1 correction in count_dependent_verdicts; the genuine turn-1 body rate is 32.3%.

- Coverage PASS. wide_boards is declared on an ATTACK-THROUGH mechanism rather than a sweeper: this deck has no board wipe available in W/R at common or uncommon. Kicked Heroic Charge grants the whole team trample, which is how a board of 1/1s pushes damage through a wider board of chump blockers; Smash to Dust's third mode deals 1 damage to each creature an opponent controls; and Jaya's -2 scales with the number of attacking creatures. That is a legitimate answer for a deck that never needs to stabilise, and it is stated as such rather than dressed up as removal.


### FAILURE MODES

| Mode | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| flood | mitigation | Karn, Living Legacy '-1: Pay any amount of mana. Look at that many cards from the top of your library, then put one of those cards into your hand' is the deck's ONE ungated repeatable mana sink - 1 of 23 nonland cards, stated at that size. Valiant Veteran's '{3}{W}{W}, Exile this card from your graveyard: Put a +1/+1 counter on each Soldier you control' is a second, five-mana outlet that works from the graveyard, so a flooded draw that already lost the Veteran still has somewhere to put mana. Squee's '{3}{R} and exiling four other cards from your graveyard' recast is a third. Crystal Grotto x2 scry 1 on entry and Urza chapter I scries 4. CORRECTION OF RECORD: an earlier draft also named Phoenix Chick as 'a third repeatable use for spare mana' - it is not. Its oracle gates the {R}{R} on 'Whenever you attack with three or more creatures', a triggered ability, not an activated mana outlet, and the board state where it fires is the opposite of a flooded one. |
| screw | mitigation | 12 of the 23 nonland cards cost 2 or less, so a two-land hand deploys on turn 2 and again on turn 3. The mana base is 15 of 17 untapped across only two colours with 8 unconditional W sources and 9 unconditional R, so colour screw is close to eliminated and what remains is pure land-count screw; the goldfish check reports 3 lands by turn 3 in 88% of hands and a play by turn 2 in 95%. STATED HONESTLY: the genuine TURN-ONE deployment rate is 32.3%, not the 58% an earlier draft claimed - Hammerhand is an Aura that cannot be cast on an empty board, so Phoenix Chick x2 is the deck's only real turn-1 play. |
| decapitation | accepted | The sharpest single point of failure is not Jaya but VALIANT VETERAN - 1 of 23 cards, whose death simultaneously un-pumps every Soldier on the board. Jaya matters too and there is no second copy: the pool contains exactly 4 planeswalkers, all mythic, and the 5-card rare/mythic cap forbids redundancy at any price. What limits the damage is that neither is the win condition - the deck's damage comes from an unconditional floor of 7 Soldier bodies plus Squee's per-attack Goblins plus Jaya's Monks, and kicked Heroic Charge's trample, none of which needs a planeswalker to function. Mitigating properly would mean either a second walker (requiring a third colour for Liliana or a hard green pip for Ajani, wrecking a two-colour mana base built for speed) or protection spells like Take Up the Shield, which cost a card to save one creature in a deck whose value is quantity. Accepted at that cost. |
| gas-out | accepted | The mana audit reports cantrip_count 0 and this deck deliberately runs zero card-filtering - no Thrill of Possibility, no Goblin Picker - because the shape judge's stated reason for rejecting the sweeper-resilient build was that board-neutral turns push an aggressor's clock past 7. What refuels instead is PRODUCTION rather than draw: Jaya's +1 makes a body every turn indefinitely, Squee makes a free Goblin on every attack and can be recast from the graveyard, Baird makes a free Soldier at every end step once any pump is active, Phoenix Chick returns itself from the graveyard, and Valiant Veteran's graveyard ability turns a dead card into a mass pump. Karn's -1 and Urza chapter I are the only true card-selection, 2 of 23. The cost of mitigating - adding card draw - is turns this deck does not have, and that is the trade the lens was chosen to make. |
| raced | mitigation | This deck IS the fast clock - goldfish turn 7, average MV 2.61. Against another aggressor it has Lightning Strike x2, whose 'deals 3 damage to any target' genuinely can go to the face, plus Hurloon Battle Hymn kicked for 4 damage and 4 life. CORRECTION OF RECORD: an earlier draft called Hurloon 'an 8-point swing'. Its oracle reads 'deals 4 damage to target creature or planeswalker' - the damage CANNOT hit the opponent, so the swing is 4 life gained plus one attacker killed, which is real against a creature-based aggressor but is not 8 points of reach against a burn or evasive clock. The sideboard adds a second copy for exactly the creature-based case. |
| disruption-fizzle | mitigation | There is no single critical turn. The deck's damage is distributed across many small permanents rather than concentrated in one spell, so a counterspell or removal spell answers roughly one seventh of the board, and Squee can be recast from the graveyard after being answered once. The two spells that DO represent a critical turn are Heroic Charge x2, and the mitigation is simple redundancy: there are two of them. Resolute Reinforcements has Flash, so the deck can deploy after the opponent commits. If a Heroic Charge is countered the board is still on the battlefield and simply attacks again next turn, which is the structural advantage of a permanent-based clock over a spell-based one. CORRECTION OF RECORD: an earlier draft claimed 'Hammerhand x2 provides a cheaper alternate route' to the same effect. It does not - 'When this Aura enters, target creature can't block this turn' is a one-shot ETB removing ONE blocker, whereas kicked Heroic Charge gives the whole team trample. Against three blockers Hammerhand removes one. |
