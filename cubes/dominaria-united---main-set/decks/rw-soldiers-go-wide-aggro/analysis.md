## ANALYSIS

### DECK IDENTITY

RW Go-Wide Soldiers. The deck converts card count into board count and board count into lethal. Resolute Reinforcements, Argivian Cavalier, Captain's Call and kicked Keldon Strike Team each turn one card into two or three bodies. Valiant Veteran's 'Other Soldiers you control get +1/+1' and Charismatic Vanguard's '{4}{W}: Creatures you control get +1/+1 until end of turn' raise that swarm, and Heroic Charge - an INSTANT, cast after blockers and after the opponent's removal - gives the team +2/+1 and, kicked for {1}{R}, trample. STATED PLAINLY, because both grill agents pressed on it: Valiant Veteran is a TOKEN anthem, not a creature anthem. Only 6 of the 14 nontoken creature copies are Soldiers; Phoenix Chick is a Phoenix, Argivian Cavalier an Orc Knight, Keldon Strike Team a Human Warrior. What it reliably buffs is the token stream, every member of which is a 1/1 white Soldier. Equipment is a two-card sub-theme and nothing more - see equipment_theme_disclosure.

### THIS IS THE LEAST FAITHFUL OF THE THREE DECKS TO THE BRIEF, AND THAT IS STATED UP FRONT

Two copies of Vanquisher's Axe = **2 of 23 nonland cards (8.7%)**. No Vehicles, no Astor, no Equipment tutor, no equipped-creature-matters payoff. Nothing in this list cares that the Axe is an Equipment rather than any other permanent granting +2/+0. Against an "Equipment & Vehicles" brief this build fails theme fidelity outright; its defence is that it is the strongest raw RW shell in the cube, not that it answers the brief. It is included because you asked for all three paths built.

### VALIANT VETERAN IS A TOKEN ANTHEM, NOT A CREATURE ANTHEM

This is the count that matters most and it is worse than it looks. *"Other Soldiers you control get +1/+1."* Reading every type line in the list:

| Receives the anthem | Does not |
|---|---|
| Baird, Argivian Recruiter x2 — Human **Soldier** | Phoenix Chick — **Phoenix** |
| Resolute Reinforcements x2 — Human **Soldier** | Argivian Cavalier x2 — Orc **Knight** |
| Charismatic Vanguard x2 — Dwarf **Soldier** | Keldon Strike Team x2 — Human **Warrior** |
| Guardian of New Benalia — Human **Soldier** | |

That is **7 of 14 nontoken creature copies**, and "Other" excludes Valiant Veteran itself, so 6 actually get pumped. Baird is legendary, so on board the practical figure is 5. What the anthem reliably buffs is the **token stream** — every token this deck makes is a 1/1 white Soldier, from Resolute Reinforcements x2, Argivian Cavalier x2, Captain's Call x2, kicked Keldon Strike Team x2 and Baird's end-step trigger. Judge the card as a token lord, not a tribal lord.

### BAIRD'S CONDITION IS NOT AMBIENT

*"if you control a creature with power greater than its base power"* — 9 of 23 copies can satisfy it, but exactly **one** does so for free and standing: Valiant Veteran, whose anthem is a continuous effect, so every other Soldier permanently has power above base. The other eight all cost something: Vanquisher's Axe x2 (MV 1 plus Equip {2} = 3 mana total), Charismatic Vanguard x2 (five mana, and it must be activated in main phase two to still be live at the end step), Argivian Cavalier x2 (requires attacking, and enlisting subtracts a body from the alpha strike), Heroic Charge x2 (the finisher — burning it to feed Baird is not a real line).

### HEROIC CHARGE'S INSTANT SPEED IS THE WHOLE FINISH

*"Creatures you control get +2/+1 until end of turn. If this spell was kicked, those creatures also gain trample until end of turn."* No type restriction, so unlike the anthem it covers all 14 creature copies **and** every token. Being an instant is load-bearing three ways: it is held until after blockers are declared, it is held until after the opponent has spent removal, and killing one attacker in response subtracts a fraction of a lethal count rather than the plan. Kicked for {1}{R} the trample clause routes damage past the chump blocks a 1/1 swarm otherwise dies to — at six mana total, which is a turn-6 play against a turn-5 thesis.

### THE SLOT TABLE REPORTS THE WORSE NUMBER ON PURPOSE

Threats/Payoffs is 39.1%, **below** the 45–55% aggro band. It could have been reported at 65.2% by filing the token producers as threats instead of enablers. Both classifications are defensible — in a go-wide deck the token producers *are* the threat, and the cube's own taxonomy tags them `Enabler/Fodder` because a single 1/1 is not individually a threat. What is not defensible is picking whichever bucketing lands inside the band, which is what the pre-grill record did (Captain's Call filed as a threat while two cards with an identical taxonomic profile were filed as residual). The taxonomy-consistent figure is reported here, deviation and all.

### FAILURE MODES

| Mode | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| flood | mitigation | The best flood deck of the three, because its sinks are repeatable and scale with the board it already has: Charismatic Vanguard x2 '{4}{W}: Creatures you control get +1/+1 until end of turn' has no once-per-turn clause, so with 6-8 bodies out it is +6/+6 or more per activation and can be activated repeatedly; Valiant Veteran '{3}{W}{W}, Exile this card from your graveyard: Put a +1/+1 counter on each Soldier you control' (permanent counters, from the graveyard); Phoenix Chick's {R}{R} return; Keldon Strike Team's kicker {1}{W}; Heroic Charge's kicker {1}{R}; and Equip {2}. |
| screw | mitigation | REWRITTEN after Challenger #1, which was valid and BLOCKING. The pre-repair entry admitted the deck had zero card selection and justified it as 'the price of the flat curve' - and that justification was FALSE, because the pool contains R/W selection at MV 2 or below that costs the curve nothing. Two such cards are now maindecked: Samite Herbalist ('Whenever this creature becomes tapped, you gain 1 life and scry 1' - it taps on every attack, and this deck attacks from turn 3 onward every turn) and Guardian of New Benalia ('Whenever this creature enlists a creature, scry 2'). The structural half of the mitigation stands and was independently verified: 13 of 23 spells cost 2 or less, nothing costs more than 4, and goldfish reports 86% keepable and 88% three-lands-by-turn-3. |
| decapitation | mitigation | There is no key card to answer. The payoff role is 5 copies across three different cards (Valiant Veteran, Charismatic Vanguard x2, Heroic Charge x2, p=0.80 by turn 5) and the enabler role is 9 copies across five cards (p=0.95), so removing any single permanent leaves the plan intact: kill Valiant Veteran and Charismatic Vanguard x2 plus Heroic Charge x2 still convert the swarm; kill Baird and the four token-producing cards still build it. Disclosed rather than hidden: Baird is legendary, so its 2 copies are 1 body, and the Engine bucket is thin at 3 of 23. |
| gas-out | mitigation | CORRECTED per Challenger #8, which found the pre-repair count of 'ten' double-counted gated modes. Baird still refuels with an empty hand and no mana when that condition holds. Phoenix Chick recurs from the graveyard and Valiant Veteran's graveyard ability is a second late-game outlet. |
| raced | accepted | Mitigating would mean maindecking Prayer of Binding x2 (MV 4) over the token producers and anthems, against a cube whose largest threat class is evasion (51 cards, 20.6%) and whose lifegain class is 22 cards. CORRECTED per Challenger #10: the pre-repair entry declined Hurloon Battle Hymn on the same curve ground, which does not apply to it - Hurloon is MV 3, squarely inside the curve - so it is now IN the sideboard, where its 4 damage answers the toughness-4 blockers Lightning Strike cannot and its kicked mode gains 4 life. The deck still accepts being outraced by a dedicated flier deck and keeps only Lightning Strike x2 in the main. |
| disruption-fizzle | mitigation | The critical turn is the Heroic Charge alpha strike, and three properties protect it, all verified against oracle text. (1) Heroic Charge is an INSTANT: it is held until after blockers are declared and after the opponent has committed removal, so a removal spell cannot pre-empt it. (2) Its effect is 'Creatures you control get +2/+1', board-wide across 5-8 attackers, so killing one attacker in response subtracts a fraction of a lethal count, not the plan. (3) Resolute Reinforcements x2 has Flash, so bodies can be added at instant speed after interaction is spent. Kicked for {1}{R} the trample clause routes damage past a chump block, which is the other way this turn fizzles. |
