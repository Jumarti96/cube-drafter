# BR Goblin Recursion Sacrifice (Hordemaster/Squee)

Black-red aggro whose threats refuse to stay dead: Phoenix Chick returns every wide attack, Squee, Dubious Monarch recasts himself from the graveyard, and Cult Conscript rebuys for two mana any turn something died. Goblin Picker and Thrill of Possibility turn dead cards into gas (or bin the recursive threats on purpose), Rundvelt Hordemaster pumps the Goblin squad and filters cards on every Goblin death, and Garna plus Warhost's Frenzy convert the constant dying into raw card advantage and reach.

## ANALYSIS

**The recursion suite is the real engine — discard is card advantage here.** Thrill of Possibility and Goblin Picker binning Phoenix Chick or Squee costs nothing: Phoenix returns tapped and attacking any turn you attack with three creatures and pay {R}{R} (routine by turn 3–4 with 17 creatures plus Squee/Lagomos tokens), and Squee recasts from the graveyard for {3}{R} exiling four cards. Cult Conscript rebuys after any non-Skeleton death — which in this deck is most turns.

**Play Rundvelt Hordemaster as anthem-plus-upside, not a combo engine.** The pool caps the deck at five other Goblin cards (2 Splatter Goblin, 2 Goblin Picker, Squee), so the death-trigger exile hits a castable Goblin only ~15% of the time. What it reliably does: +1/+1 on Splatter Goblins, Pickers, Squee and every Squee token, and a free card-filter ping whenever any of them (tokens included) dies. Note Lagomos's tokens are Elementals — they feed sac outlets and Garna, but never Hordemaster.

**Garna splits deaths into two good modes:** attackers that die (bad blocks, mid-combat Hurler Cyclops sacs, expired Phoenix trades) draw cards; everything else (Lagomos's end-step token, Bone Splinters fodder) pings each opponent. With Warhost's Frenzy kicked, an all-in attack refuels the hand even when the board trades off — the classic line is Frenzy kicked, attack with everything, sac leftovers to Hurler Cyclops after blocks.

**Defiler of Instinct rewards the recursion loop:** every red permanent spell — including Squee recast from the graveyard and Hordemaster exile-casts — pings any target, clearing 1-toughness blockers so the squad keeps connecting. Its life-payment discount effectively makes red permanents cost {R} less, fueling double-spell turns.

**Mana note (grill-adjusted):** the deck went from 14 to 15 lands at the Challenger's recommendation — the curve is low (avg 2.32) but Squee recasts, {R}{R} Phoenix returns and kicked Frenzy are real mana sinks, and black (9 pips / 8 sources) needed the extra Swamp for turn-1 Conscript and Bone Splinters.

### Cards Considered but Excluded

**Rares/mythics cut or left out (deck sits at 4 of 5 — one slot deliberately open):**
- The Elder Dragon War — held the fifth slot until the grill: chapter I's 2 damage to each creature kills our own tokens, Phoenix and most of the board. Replaced by Cut Down.
- Ragefire Hellkite — the natural fifth rare if you want a top-end: flying sac-outlet finisher, fully on-plan. Add it over Hurler Cyclops if your meta goes long.
- Shivan Devastator — hasty X-flier; generically excellent aggro mana sink with zero synergy text. The other candidate for the open slot.
- Liliana of the Veil / Braids, Arisen Nightmare — premium black cards, but this build is too aggressive to want symmetric attrition.
- Radha's Firebrand — solid aggro 2-drop, lost the slot to Goblin typing and recursion.

**Strong uncommons/commons a tier below the includes:**
- Knight of Dusk's Shadow — cut at the grill for Garna: pure beater with no death/recursion text.
- Battlefly Swarm — cut at the grill for the 15th land; re-add if you shave a land back.
- Phyrexian Vivisector — cheap death-scry body; first non-rare addition if you want a 18th creature.
- Balduvian Atrocity — kicked mini-reanimation with haste that then sacs itself; on-plan, competes at 3.
- Flowstone Kavu / Dragon Whelp / Toxic Abomination — bodies without pipeline text.

**Sideboard-consideration cards:** Furious Bellow (trick + scry vs aggro mirrors), Extinguish the Light (unconditional removal if you add a 6th Swamp), Yavimaya Steelcrusher (enlist body that sacs for artifact kill — overlaps Smash to Dust).
