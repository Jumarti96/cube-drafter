## ANALYSIS

**Deck identity.** Gruul ramp-midrange that converts five true accelerants (2 Deathbloom Gardener, 1 Llanowar Loamspeaker, 2 The Weatherseed Treaty) plus land-drop insurance (2 kicked Sprouting Goblin, Rulik Mons' attack trigger) into oversized threats: Shivan Devastator X, Defiler of Vigor's board-wide snowball, Briar Hydra, and a discounted Molten Monstrosity. Six mainboard kicker cards keep the DMU kicker theme live at aggressive rates.

**The Molten Monstrosity math.** Its cost is {7}{R} minus the greatest power among your creatures, checked on cast. Curve lines that matter: Defiler of Vigor or Briar Hydra on board (power 6) makes it {1}{R}; a kicked Viashino Branchrider (3/3) makes it {4}{R}; Llanowar Loamspeaker can animate a land into a 3/3 at sorcery speed *before* you cast it — a hidden discount the opponent rarely plays around.

**Defiler of Vigor as the engine.** Fourteen green permanent spells trigger it. Each one adds a counter to every creature you control, and the pay-2-life discount lets you chain two green spells a turn — Nishoba Brawler and Yavimaya Iconoclast get big fast under it.

**Removal scaling.** Bite Down is one-sided (your creature takes nothing back): with 6-power bodies it is two-mana instant-speed kill-anything. Lightning Strike covers early plays; Jaya's Firenado handles opposing fatties.

**Domain honesty.** This manabase has exactly 2 basic land types, so the domain cards run at their floor: Nishoba Brawler is a fixed 2/3 trampler, Briar Hydra adds 2 counters per connect, The Weatherseed Treaty's chapter III grants +2/+2 and trample. All three were kept for their bodies/effects at that floor, not their ceilings — roles are labeled accordingly.

**Grill outcome.** Challenger found no blockers and ruled the pipeline viable, with three majors, all adopted: 2x Salvaged Manaworker (a {1}-in-1-out filter, useless in strict two-color) → 2x The Weatherseed Treaty (true battlefield ramp); Territorial Maro (a vanilla 4/4 for 5 at domain 2) → Rulik Mons, Warren Chief (the Challenger's named best on-plan card); sideboard 2x Tail Swipe (weak when your early creatures are small) → 2x Flowstone Infusion. Four role labels were rewritten for honesty (Nishoba, Iconoclast, Monstrosity, Sprouting Goblin).

**Cards Considered but Excluded.**
- *Rares/mythics cut by the 5-card cap:* Quirion Beastcaller (creature-cast counters engine — the natural 6th rare), Squee, Dubious Monarch (recursive aggro, better in the BR build), Keldon Flamesage (wants a spells build), Meria, Scholar of Antiquity (wants artifacts), Jaya, Fiery Negotiator (mythic value walker, strong but off-pipeline), Karplusan Forest (untapped RG rare land — first swap-in if you free a rare slot), Thran Portal (could add a third domain type at the cost of a rare slot), Ragefire Hellkite / The Elder Dragon War / Chaotic Transformation (top-end alternatives a tier below the chosen finishers here).
- *Strong uncommons a tier below:* Linebreaker Baloth (fine beater, lost the 5-drop slot to Defiler of Vigor), Slimefoot's Survey (real ramp but 5 MV in a deck whose payoffs start at 5), Radha, Coalition Warlord (+2/+2 per tap at domain 2), Electrostatic Infantry / Twinferno (spellslinger-facing), Balduvian Berserker, Dragon Whelp, Hurler Cyclops (off-pipeline).
- *Sideboard considerations:* Tail Swipe (cut by grill — true fight, our early bodies lose it), Magnigoth Sentry and Hexbane Tortoise (defensive filler), Furious Bellow, Thrill of Possibility (draw at card disadvantage without a graveyard theme).

**Matchup notes.** Strong against ground midrange (bigger bodies, one-sided fights) and control that leans on spot removal (Devastator's haste and Treaty's token/pump chapters give resilience). Weak to fliers (board Broken Wings + Snarespinner) and to go-wide aggro (Smash to Dust's ping mode plus Flowstone Infusion).
