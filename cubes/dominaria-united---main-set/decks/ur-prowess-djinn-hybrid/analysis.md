## ANALYSIS

**Deck identity.** The hybrid of the prowess-aggro and spell-velocity builds: a Balmor, Battlemage Captain attack shell (seven spell-cast payoff bodies) whose same cheap-instant suite simultaneously fills the graveyard for Haughty Djinn and Tolarian Terror. The Challenger's key verdict: this is not two half-decks — every cheap instant triggers Balmor/Infantry/Amplifier now AND discounts Terror / grows Djinn later. Terror is a tempo threat here (a 4-5 mana 5/5 ward on turn 4-5), not a control finisher.

**Grill outcomes applied.** Three swaps from the Challenger: Furious Bellow → Haunting Figment (an unblockable Balmor carrier beats a combat trick in a creature-light deck), Djinn of the Fountain → Phoenix Chick (the deck's only true 1-drop; also removes the second hard {4}{U}{U} six-drop from a 15-land base), and Fires of Victory → Keldon Flamesage (both grill agents endorsed spending an open rare slot on it — a free-cast instant on attack is a free Balmor trigger plus a free graveyard card).

**Flamesage note.** Keldon Flamesage is a base 2/2, so its attack trigger sees MV ≤ 2 unaided — which is fine here: 11 of the deck's 12 instants/sorceries are MV ≤ 2. Enlist an idle body only when you want to dig deeper.

**Mana honesty (from the grill).** Turn-2 Balmor lands roughly 50-60% of games (9 effective untapped U sources, 8 R); plan around a turn-3 Balmor into an open-mana turn instead. Sequence Molten Tributary on turn 1 whenever possible. True interaction is Lightning Strike ×2 + Essence Scatter + sideboard — Timely Interference is trigger fuel, not removal; the deck races and protects rather than answers.

**Cosmic Epiphany caveat.** Hard {4}{U}{U} with no self-discount on 15 lands is a turn-7+ play; it is the deck's win-the-grind button, dead in fast matchups — the first card to board out against aggro.

### Cards Considered but Excluded

**Rares/mythics cut for the 5-card budget (4/5 used — one slot open):**
- Silver Scrutiny — the Challenger's suggested flexible alternative to Cosmic Epiphany (instant-speed draw X, flash if X≤3); the natural fill for the open 5th slot, or a straight Epiphany swap if your pod is fast.
- Squee, Dubious Monarch / Shivan Devastator — the pure-aggro rares; they belong to the dedicated prowess-aggro build.
- Jaya, Fiery Negotiator — token engine better served in the big-spell midrange build.

**Strong uncommons a tier below the chosen includes:**
- Djinn of the Fountain — cut in the grill: modal but small for 6 mana, no discount from either engine, and the second hard double-blue 6-drop the mana couldn't afford.
- Battlewing Mystic — kicked refuel flier; fights the hold-up-instants plan.
- Najal, the Storm Runner — copy effects want haymakers; this deck's spells are deliberately small.
- Academy Wall — pure defense; this list wants its 3-drops attacking.

**Sideboard-consideration cards that missed the 10:**
- Furious Bellow — cut from main in the grill; board it back vs removal-light decks as extra reach.
- Fires of Victory — cut from main; strong vs planeswalkers if your pod runs them.
- Ertai's Scorn — third counterspell vs control.
