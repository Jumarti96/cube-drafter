# Dominaria Remastered — Main Set: Draft Archetypes

**Mode:** Guided Coverage — *"link all rares and mythics to at least one archetype if possible; if a rare/mythic truly doesn't fit an archetype (or is sideboard material), leave it out honestly."* Parsed filter: `rarity in {rare, mythic}` (80 mainboard cards).

Tag coverage going in: all 261 mainboard cards tagged. Coverage result: **74/80 rares & mythics placed**; 6 reported unplaceable with reasons (see appendix).


## Core

### Goblins — Core
**Colors:** R (B splash)  **Macro lean:** Aggro
**Keystones:** [Pashalik Mons](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pashalik+Mons"), [Siege-Gang Commander](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Siege-Gang+Commander")
**Core cards:** [Skirk Prospector](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Skirk+Prospector"), [Goblin Matron](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Goblin+Matron"), [Mogg War Marshal](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mogg+War+Marshal"), [Gempalm Incinerator](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gempalm+Incinerator"), [Dralnu's Crusade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dralnu's+Crusade"), [Empty the Warrens](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Empty+the+Warrens"), [Cryptic Gateway](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cryptic+Gateway"), [Urza's Incubator](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Urza's+Incubator") (+6 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Tribal/Kindred; Aristocrats/Sacrifice; Tokens
**Why it works:** Pashalik Mons turns every Goblin death into damage and Skirk Prospector turns them into mana; Dralnu's Crusade ("All Goblins are black and are Zombies") lets Deadapult ("Sacrifice a Zombie: ...deals 2 damage") eat any Goblin, while Urza's Incubator and Cryptic Gateway cheat expensive Goblins like Siege-Gang Commander into play; Sulfuric Vortex is the cross-tag reach finisher that also shuts off opposing lifegain.

### Aristocrats/Sacrifice — Core
**Colors:** B (R/W secondary)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Yawgmoth, Thran Physician](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Yawgmoth,+Thran+Physician"), [Oversold Cemetery](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Oversold+Cemetery")
**Core cards:** [Phyrexian Ghoul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Phyrexian+Ghoul"), [Phyrexian Debaser](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Phyrexian+Debaser"), [Zombie Infestation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zombie+Infestation"), [Mogg War Marshal](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mogg+War+Marshal"), [Symbiotic Beast](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Symbiotic+Beast"), [Penumbra Bobcat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Penumbra+Bobcat"), [Necrosavant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Necrosavant"), [Pyre Zombie](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pyre+Zombie") (+8 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Aristocrats/Sacrifice; Tokens; Graveyard
**Why it works:** Yawgmoth converts spare bodies into cards plus -1/-1 removal while Oversold Cemetery ("if you have four or more creature cards in your graveyard... return target creature card... to your hand") rebuys the fodder; cross-tag: Spinal Embrace steals a blocker you can feed to Phyrexian Ghoul before its own end-step sacrifice, and Mindslicer's death dumps hands when yours is already empty.

### Threshold — Core
**Colors:** GW (U/B enablers)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Divine Sacrament](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Divine+Sacrament"), [Hunting Grounds](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hunting+Grounds")
**Core cards:** [Mystic Enforcer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mystic+Enforcer"), [Werebear](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Werebear"), [Nantuko Monastery](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nantuko+Monastery"), [Terravore](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Terravore"), [Glory](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Glory"), [Vigilant Sentry](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vigilant+Sentry"), [Mystic Zealot](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mystic+Zealot"), [Nomad Decoy](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nomad+Decoy") (+9 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Graveyard; Self-Mill; Delirium; Flashback/GY-Cast
**Why it works:** Divine Sacrament, Mystic Enforcer, Werebear, Nantuko Monastery and friends all upgrade once "there are seven or more cards in your graveyard", and the cube's 20+ cycling cards plus Frantic Search/Millikin/Street Wraith fill the yard while replacing themselves; Glory grants color protection while sitting in the graveyard.

### Enchantress — Core
**Colors:** WG (UB via Zur)  **Macro lean:** Midrange/Control
**Keystones:** [Mesa Enchantress](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mesa+Enchantress"), [Zur the Enchanter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Zur+the+Enchanter")
**Core cards:** [Sylvan Library](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sylvan+Library"), [Enlightened Tutor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Enlightened+Tutor"), [Auramancer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Auramancer"), [Sevinne's Reclamation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sevinne's+Reclamation"), [Mystic Remora](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mystic+Remora"), [Hunting Grounds](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hunting+Grounds"), [Squirrel Nest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Squirrel+Nest"), [Divine Sacrament](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Divine+Sacrament") (+3 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Enchantress; Voltron/Equipment; Stax/Taxing
**Why it works:** Mesa Enchantress draws a card per enchantment cast and Zur the Enchanter puts any enchantment "with mana value 3 or less" straight onto the battlefield — Mystic Remora (1), Hunting Grounds (2), Sylvan Library (2), Divine Sacrament (3), Squirrel Nest (3); Sevinne's Reclamation and Auramancer rebuy them from the graveyard.

### Tokens / Go-Wide — Core
**Colors:** GW (R)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Opposition](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Opposition"), [Saproling Symbiosis](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Saproling+Symbiosis")
**Core cards:** [Battle Screech](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Battle+Screech"), [Call of the Herd](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Call+of+the+Herd"), [Squirrel Nest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Squirrel+Nest"), [Nut Collector](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nut+Collector"), [Symbiotic Beast](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Symbiotic+Beast"), [Jolrael, Mwonvuli Recluse](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Jolrael,+Mwonvuli+Recluse"), [Empty the Warrens](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Empty+the+Warrens"), [Mogg War Marshal](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mogg+War+Marshal") (+5 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Tokens; Tribal/Kindred; Stax/Taxing
**Why it works:** Opposition converts token width into a full tap-down lock; Saproling Symbiosis, Battle Screech, and Squirrel Nest go wide while Divine Sacrament pumps the white tokens, Congregate ("2 life for each creature") and Kavu Primarch's convoke cash the width in, and Nut Collector's threshold makes every Squirrel a 3/3.

### Reanimator / Cheat Big Creatures — Core
**Colors:** B (R)  **Macro lean:** Combo/Midrange
**Keystones:** [Chainer, Dementia Master](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chainer,+Dementia+Master"), [Sneak Attack](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sneak+Attack")
**Core cards:** [Entomb](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Entomb"), [Body Snatcher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Body+Snatcher"), [Dread Return](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dread+Return"), [Life // Death](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Life+//+Death"), [Worldgorger Dragon](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Worldgorger+Dragon"), [Gamble](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gamble"), [Vampiric Tutor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vampiric+Tutor"), [Worldly Tutor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Worldly+Tutor") (+7 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Reanimator; Graveyard; Self-Mill
**Why it works:** Entomb and Gamble (tutor plus random discard) stock the graveyard; Chainer, Dread Return, and Life // Death return the bodies while Sneak Attack cheats them in from hand; cross-tag: Worldly Tutor + Gamekeeper ("reveal cards from the top of your library until you reveal a creature card. Put that card onto the battlefield") cheats any creature from the library, and Arcanis, Denizen of the Deep, and Phantom Nishoba are untagged but textbook targets (huge bodies or a repeatable "{T}: Draw three cards").

## Build-Around

### Marit Lage (Dark Depths) — Build-Around
**Colors:** C (G support)  **Macro lean:** Combo
**Keystones:** [Dark Depths](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dark+Depths")
**Core cards:** [Crop Rotation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Crop+Rotation"), [Exploration](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Exploration"), [Krosan Restorer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Krosan+Restorer"), [Vampiric Tutor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vampiric+Tutor")
**Key themes:** Lands-Matter; Combo
**Why it works:** Dark Depths makes a legendary 20/20 flying indestructible token once its ten ice counters are gone; Crop Rotation ("Search your library for a land card, put that card onto the battlefield") fetches it, Exploration replays the land drops it eats, and Krosan Restorer's threshold untap helps pay the {3}-per-counter cost. Single-card anchor — speculate only with support.

### Mono-Color Big Mana (Gauntlet) — Build-Around
**Colors:** any single color (B/R best)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Gauntlet of Power](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gauntlet+of+Power")
**Core cards:** [Nantuko Shade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Nantuko+Shade"), [Shivan Dragon](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shivan+Dragon"), [Avarax](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Avarax"), [Dragon Whelp](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dragon+Whelp"), [Glintwing Invoker](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Glintwing+Invoker"), [Stonewood Invoker](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Stonewood+Invoker"), [Howl from Beyond](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Howl+from+Beyond"), [High Tide](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"High+Tide") (+1 more supporting this archetype)
**Key themes:** Payload/Payoff mana sinks; Tribal color-pump
**Why it works:** Gauntlet of Power pumps the chosen color and makes every basic of that color "add an additional one mana"; the cube is full of firebreathing-style sinks to spend it — Nantuko Shade and Shivan Dragon pump per mana, Howl from Beyond is an X-spell, and the Invokers carry 7-mana activations.

### Untap Pinger Engine (Crab Gun) — Build-Around
**Colors:** U (R)  **Macro lean:** Combo/Control
**Keystones:** [Horseshoe Crab](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Horseshoe+Crab"), [Hermetic Study](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hermetic+Study")
**Core cards:** [Quicksilver Dagger](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Quicksilver+Dagger"), [Opposition](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Opposition")
**Key themes:** Enchantress (aura payoffs); Engine/Outlet
**Why it works:** Horseshoe Crab's "{U}: Untap this creature" repeats any tap ability: with Hermetic Study ("{T}: This creature deals 1 damage to any target") every {U} is a ping, with Quicksilver Dagger it is a ping plus a card, and with Opposition every {U} taps an opposing permanent. Two-card engine — draft the pieces together.
