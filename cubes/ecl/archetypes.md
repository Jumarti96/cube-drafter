# ECL Set Cube — Archetype Map

**Mode:** Guided Coverage
**Parsed filter:** *at least one archetype must exist for each of the ten two-colour pairs (WU, UB, BR, RG, GW, WB, BG, GU, UR, RW), plus every other archetype identifiable in the pool.*
**Pool:** 278 mainboard cards, **0 untagged** going in.
**Coverage result:** all ten pairs covered — see the pair index below. 20 archetypes reported.

The cube's skeleton is explicit: the **five allied pairs each get a transforming legend plus a "Command"** (Brigid/GW, Sygg/UW, Ashling/UR, Grub/BR, Trystan/BG) and own one of the five signpost tribes. The **five enemy pairs each get an Incarnation** (Catharsis/RW, Deceit/UB, Emptiness/WB, Vibrance/RG, Wistfulness/GU) and a mechanical theme instead of a tribe. Faeries, Giants and Treefolk are the three secondary tribes and sit on top of the enemy-pair themes.

**Key themes:** Tribal/Kindred, Tokens, Blink/ETB, behold
**Why it works:** 21 typed Kithkin plus six cards that produce or reference Kithkin without the type. The archetype is really two engines stapled together: a token flood (Brigid's front face, Clachan Festival, Ajani's +1, Kinbinding's end-of-combat token) and a **"a creature entered this turn"** payoff suite that the tagger did not label as Kithkin — [Crossroads Watcher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Crossroads+Watcher") ("Whenever another creature you control enters, this creature gets +1/+0"), [Bristlebane Outrider](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bristlebane+Outrider") ("As long as another creature entered the battlefield under your control this turn, this creature gets +2/+0"), [Thoughtweft Charge](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Thoughtweft+Charge") ("If a creature entered the battlefield under your control this turn, draw a card") and [Wary Farmer](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wary+Farmer"). Kinbinding converts the whole thing into a scaling anthem: "Creatures you control get +X/+X, where X is the number of creatures that entered the battlefield under your control this turn."

**Key themes:** Tribal/Kindred, Tapped Matters, convoke, Tokens
**Why it works:** 21 Merfolk, and the pair's real engine is **tapping your own creatures for value** — five cards read "Whenever this creature becomes tapped" ([Silvergill Peddler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Silvergill+Peddler") loots, [Pestered Wellguard](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pestered+Wellguard") makes a Faerie, [Tributary Vaulter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Tributary+Vaulter") pumps, [Wanderbrine Preacher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wanderbrine+Preacher") gains 2, [Wanderwine Distracter](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wanderwine+Distracter") shrinks a blocker) while 16 convoke cards and [Wanderbrine Trapper](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Wanderbrine+Trapper") supply the tap outlets. Untappers close the loop: Deepway Navigator "untap each other Merfolk you control", Deepchannel Duelist untaps one each end step. Adept Watershaper — tagged only `Tapped Matters`, no Merfolk synergy tag — makes the whole tapped board indestructible, which is the payoff that turns tapping out from a drawback into an attrition plan.

### Blight / −1/−1 Counters — Core
**Colors:** B/R/W primary, present in all five colours  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Shadow Urchin](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shadow+Urchin"), [Rhys, the Evermore](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Rhys,+the+Evermore"), [Lasting Tarfire](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lasting+Tarfire")
**Core cards:** [Slumbering Walker](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Slumbering+Walker"), [Bristlebane Battler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bristlebane+Battler"), [Loch Mare](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Loch+Mare"), [Moonshadow](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Moonshadow"), [Reluctant Dounguard](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Reluctant+Dounguard"), [Spinerock Tyrant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Spinerock+Tyrant"), [Blighted Blackthorn](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Blighted+Blackthorn"), [Warren Torchmaster](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Warren+Torchmaster") (53 cards in the counters cluster)
**Key themes:** Counters, blight, wither, persist, Counter Removal
**Why it works:** This is the set's signature mechanic and the largest non-tribal cluster (53 tagged cards). It has three interlocking halves. **Discounted bodies** — 16 creatures "enter with" −1/−1 counters and are grossly above rate for their cost (Bristlebane Battler is a 6/6 for {1}{G} with five counters; Encumbered Reejerey a 5/4 for {1}{W}). **Shedding engines** that pay off density elsewhere: Reluctant Dounguard and Bristlebane Battler shed a counter whenever another creature enters, Encumbered Reejerey when it taps, Moonshadow whenever permanents hit your graveyard, Creakwood Safewright off Elves in the yard. **Blight-as-a-cost**, where putting counters on your own creatures buys effects: 25 cards use the word, including [Cinder Strike](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cinder+Strike"), [Bogslither's Embrace](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bogslither's+Embrace"), [Pyrrhic Strike](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Pyrrhic+Strike"), [Soul Immolation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Soul+Immolation") and [Scuzzback Scrounger](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Scuzzback+Scrounger"). Shadow Urchin is the standalone payoff ("Whenever a creature you control with one or more counters on it dies, exile that many cards from the top of your library… you may play those cards"), Lasting Tarfire pings for 2 on any turn you place a counter, and Rhys plus [Perfect Intimidation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Perfect+Intimidation") clean the counters off afterwards.

**Why it works:** The WB Incarnation and the WB god define one deck: recur cheap creatures repeatedly. Four effects return small bodies straight to the battlefield — Emptiness ("return target creature card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to the battlefield" on the {W}{W} mode), Reaping Willow (same clause, repeatable by removing counters), Meanders Guide (same, on attack), Slumbering Walker ("power 2 or less"). Isilu's back face grants **every other nontoken creature persist**, which is both a recursion engine and a −1/−1 counter generator; Rhys, the Evermore grants persist too and then strips the counter. Lifegain runs alongside as the pair's incidental reward — 23 cards gain life, and Bre of Clan Stoutarm converts it into cards ("if you gained life this turn, exile cards from the top of your library until you exile a nonland card… you may cast that card").

### Draw-Denial Stax — Build-Around
**Colors:** B  **Macro lean:** Control/Combo
**Keystones:** [Mornsong Aria](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Mornsong+Aria")
**Core cards:** [Rooftop Percher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Rooftop+Percher"), [Dawnhand Dissident](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dawnhand+Dissident"), [Perfect Intimidation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Perfect+Intimidation"), [Auntie's Sentence](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Auntie's+Sentence"), [Taster of Wares](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Taster+of+Wares"), [Darkness Descends](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Darkness+Descends")
**Why it works:** A genuine single-card anchor — Mornsong Aria is the only `Stax/Taxing` card in the cube. It reads "Players can't draw cards or gain life. At the beginning of each player's draw step, that player loses 3 life, searches their library for a card, puts it into their hand, then shuffles", so it symmetrically converts every draw step into a tutor and a 3-life clock. You break the symmetry with hand attack and graveyard denial (Auntie's Sentence, Perfect Intimidation's "exiles two cards from their hand" mode, Taster of Wares, Dawnhand Dissident's graveyard exile, Rooftop Percher) while a sweeper like Darkness Descends buys the turns. Note it also turns off your own lifegain and card draw — this is a deliberate deckbuilding cost, not an oversight.
