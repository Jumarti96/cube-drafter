## ANALYSIS
### DECK IDENTITY
Black-green graveyard midrange where lands matter IN THE GRAVEYARD rather than on the battlefield. Lluwen, Imperfect Naturalist reads '{2}{B/G}{B/G}{B/G}, {T}, Discard a land card: Create a 1/1 black and green Worm creature token for each land card in your graveyard' - so every self-mill is a Worm counter, Evolving Wilds is a land that puts ITSELF in the yard, and the land discarded to pay the cost joins the count before the ability resolves. After the self-grill the deck runs a SECOND payoff on the same resource: Morcant's Eyes x2 sacrifices for a 2/2 Elf per Elf card in the graveyard, which needs no land in hand, no tap, and no creature surviving a turn cycle. The same graveyard also sizes Moon-Vigil Adherents and Gloom Ripper, and Nameless Inversion is removal that becomes an Elf card in the yard the moment it resolves.

### THE COUNTER NOBODY CAN ATTACK

The dossier's structural census reports the cube's `graveyard_hate` as **empty**. Not thin - empty. The only card in all 282 that touches an opposing graveyard is Rooftop Percher ("exile up to two target cards from graveyards"), a 5-mana common. This deck's win condition reads a number that, in this environment, essentially cannot be interacted with.

### WHAT THE SELF-GRILL CHANGED, AND WHY IT MATTERS

The first version of this deck claimed "expected first activation: 7 Worms, range 6-9" on turn 8. The Challenger rebuilt the 40 and ran **20,000 goldfish games** with sequencing chosen in the build's favour. The result:

| Turn | Activation live at all | Worms, given it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **8** (thesis turn) | **33.6%** | mean **6.10**, median 6, p10 3, p90 9 |
| 9 | 41.6% | mean 6.60 |
| 10 | 49.4% | mean 7.05 |
| 12 | 61.4% | mean 7.95 |

The claimed 7 was a **turn-10 number**. The claimed floor of 6 was beaten by fewer than 59% of activations. And the 66% of turn-8 games with no activation at all went undisclosed.

The gate is triple, and that is the real lesson: Lluwen must have **resolved a full turn earlier** (the cost includes `{T}`), you need **five mana up**, *and* you need **a land card still in hand** to discard. That last requirement alone costs 8.7 percentage points of activation rate — and the original build had instructed the pilot to *decline* Midnight Tilling's "you may return a permanent card ... to your hand," turning off its own only cheap supply of the resource its engine eats.

So the repair attacked the **rate**, not the number:

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| **+2 Morcant's Eyes** | A second payoff on the same graveyard that needs no land in hand, no `{T}`, and no creature surviving a turn cycle |
| **+2 Changeling Wayfinder** | "put it into your hand" — the dedicated supply for the discard cost |
| **Midnight Tilling instruction reversed** | Take a land back when the hand has none |
| **−1 Moonshadow, +1 Overgrown Tomb** | Moonshadow is a 1/1 through the thesis turn (median ~4 events vs the 6 it needs); the freed rare slot bought the pool's only untapped B/G dual |

### AFTER THE REPAIR: THE RATE DOUBLED

The repair was re-simulated at 20,000 trials on the final list. At turn 8:

| Payoff | Live at turn 8 | Mean output | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lluwen's Worms | **45.0%** (was 33.6%) | 6.75 tokens | 6.75 |
| Morcant's Eyes' Elves | **40.5%** | 6.17 tokens | **12.3** |
| **Either one** | **67.4%** | — | — |

Exactly double the pre-repair rate, and Morcant's Eyes is now the *larger* payoff.

### MORCANT'S EYES IS THE BETTER LLUWEN

"{4}{G}{G}, Sacrifice this enchantment: Create X 2/2 black and green **Elf** creature tokens, where X is the number of **Elf cards** in your graveyard."

Elf cards are **16 of the 22 nonland cards** here — every creature except none, plus the changelings (Graveshifter, Nameless Inversion, Changeling Wayfinder) which are Elf cards *in every zone*, plus Trystan's Command, whose type line is literally `Kindred Sorcery — Elf`.

At the measured turn-8 mill of 11.4 cards, that is 4-6 Elf cards in the yard plus every Nameless Inversion already cast: **5-6 tokens at 2/2 = 10-12 power**, against Lluwen's measured 6 Worms = 6 power. And the tokens are **Elves**, so they feed Gloom Ripper's "number of Elves you control" — Worm tokens do not.

### THE CARD THAT PAYS TWICE

Nameless Inversion: "Changeling (This card is every creature type.) / Target creature gets +3/-3 and loses all creature types until end of turn."

Changeling applies in **every** zone, including the graveyard. So it is removal on cast, and then permanently an **Elf card in your graveyard** — feeding Gloom Ripper's X and switching on Creakwood Safewright's end-step trigger. Those jobs are sequential, not competing.

### HONEST ASSESSMENT

This is not a turn-8 combo deck. It is a **midrange deck with an uninteractable late game**: two independent payoffs reading a resource the cube has no answer for, on a curve that blocks well (deathtouch Trystan, lifelink Scarblade Scout) while the yard fills. The Worm activation is the ceiling, not the plan; Morcant's Eyes and a 13/13 trampling Moon-Vigil Adherents are the plan.

### PLAY PATTERN

| Turn | Line |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Scarblade Scout (mill 2, lifelink) or Creakwood Safewright. Lluwen on 2 is fine - mill 4 and put a **land** on top if your hand is short. |
| 3-4 | Morcant's Eyes (free upkeep surveil from here on) or Changeling Wayfinder, banking a land in hand for the activation. |
| 5-6 | Midnight Tilling at the opponent's end step - and take a land back if your hand has none. Gloom Ripper as removal-plus-body. |
| 7-9 | Whichever payoff is live: five mana plus a spare land for Lluwen's Worms, or six mana to sacrifice Morcant's Eyes for a 2/2 Elf per Elf card in the yard. Moon-Vigil Adherents counts every one of them. |

### FAILURE MODES

| Mode | Verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| flood | mitigation | This deck converts flood better than any other build in this pool: a surplus land in hand is the discard cost for Lluwen's activation, and every land that reaches the graveyard is a Worm. Midnight Tilling x2 turns dead draws into fill at instant speed, and Moon-Vigil Adherents grows with every creature card the flood mills past. |
| screw | mitigation | Evolving Wilds x2 fixes either colour, Haunted Mire x2 produces both, and Lluwen's ETB reads 'you may put a creature OR LAND card from among the milled cards on top of your library' - which is a land-drop tutor on a two-drop when you are short. The goldfish check reports 85% keepable and 92% to three lands by turn 3. Honest limit: the deck has zero mana creatures and zero ramp (accel count is 0), so a slow start is not accelerated out of, only dug out of. |
| decapitation | mitigation | Lluwen is a singleton, and this build answers that rather than accepting it: Graveshifter x2 ('return target creature card from your graveyard to your hand'), Trystan's Command ('Return one or two target permanent cards from your graveyard to your hand'), and from the sideboard Unbury (two mana) and Reaping Willow (which returns a mana-value-3-or-less creature card to the BATTLEFIELD - Lluwen is mana value 2). Being milled is not a loss here; it is a route. TWO LIMITS STATED, both raised by the Challenger and neither previously disclosed: all three maindeck rebuys return her to HAND, so a rebuy costs 4 or 6 mana plus 2 to recast plus a turn of summoning sickness before the tap ability is payable - roughly a three-turn slip. And against EXILE removal, which this pool supplies to every black deck at two mana (Bogslither's Embrace, a common this deck itself sideboards), all three are blank. Reaping Willow in the board is the answer to the first; there is no answer to the second, and the deck's real insurance against it is Morcant's Eyes x2, a second payoff on the same graveyard that does not depend on Lluwen at all. |
| gas-out | mitigation | REWRITTEN - the previous entry was contradicted by the deck's own cost line. Lluwen's activation reads 'Discard a land card', so every activation consumes a land card FROM HAND, and the previous claim that 'the deck does not need a hand' was false. Worse, the previous record instructed the pilot to decline Midnight Tilling's 'you may return a permanent card from among them to your hand', which was the deck's only cheap route to that resource. Both are fixed. Changeling Wayfinder x2 ('search your library for a basic land card, reveal it, put it into your hand') is now the dedicated supply, the Midnight Tilling instruction is reversed to take a land back whenever the hand has none, and Morcant's Eyes x2 is a second payoff that consumes NO card from hand at all. Genuine card-positive count in the repaired list: Graveshifter x2 (returns a creature card), Trystan's Command (returns one or two permanent cards), Midnight Tilling x2 (returns a permanent card from among the milled), Changeling Wayfinder x2 (fetches to hand) = 7 of 22 - and unlike the previous figure this one excludes Dawnhand Eulogist, which the Challenger correctly pointed out replaces no card. |
| raced | accepted | The first meaningful play is turn 2 in 92% of games, but both payoffs land turn 7-9 and the measured turn-8 fire rate for either is 67.4%. If she is removed in response to the ability, the ability still resolves - the Challenger verified this against the oracle as written: 'Create a 1/1 ... for each land card in your graveyard' has no reference back to Lluwen and no intervening-if clause, so it is an independent object on the stack that counts on resolution. If she is removed before it, nothing is wasted: the graveyard is untouched and only grows, so the next Lluwen activates for MORE Worms. The cube's structural_census lists graveyard_hate as EMPTY, so the resource itself cannot be attacked. Honest caveat from the grill: the vector this defends against is the one the cube does not have, while three it does have - exile removal, removal on Lluwen before she untaps for the tap ability, and targeted discard stripping the land she needs - are not addressed by this mode.
