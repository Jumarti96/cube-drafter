# Archetype Discovery — Innistrad Remastered Set + DMU Dual Lands (300 cards)

**Mode:** Guided Coverage — *"All rares and mythics linked to at least one archetype if possible; if one truly doesn't fit, don't include it."*
**Parsed filter:** `rarity in {rare, mythic}` → 97 cards. **Result: 85 placed, 12 reported unplaceable** (see bottom).
**Tag coverage going in:** 300/300 mainboard cards tagged.
**Self-grill:** ✅ all 258 referenced names verified against `enriched.json`; all cross-tag inclusions re-checked against oracle text; 1 claim corrected (Emrakul removed from Reanimator — [Through the Breach](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Through+the+Breach") puts a creature into play *without casting it*, so cast triggers don't fire); Defenders hypothesis cut (six defender bodies but no card counts defenders); Wheels cluster merged into Madness; Delirium merged into Self-Mill.

## Core

### Vampires — Core
**Colors:** BR (splash W via Edgar)  **Macro lean:** Aggro/Midrange
**Keystones:** [Olivia Voldaren](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Olivia+Voldaren"), [Edgar Markov](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Edgar+Markov"), [Bloodline Keeper // Lord of Lineage](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodline+Keeper"), [Sorin, Imperious Bloodlord](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sorin,+Imperious+Bloodlord")
**Core cards:** [Captivating Vampire](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Captivating+Vampire"), [Voldaren Bloodcaster // Bloodbat Summoner](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Voldaren+Bloodcaster"), [Indulgent Aristocrat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Indulgent+Aristocrat"), [Stensia Masquerade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Stensia+Masquerade"), [Bloodtithe Harvester](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodtithe+Harvester"), [Voldaren Ambusher](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Voldaren+Ambusher") (+15 more: Blood-token makers, madness Vampires, lords' fodder)
**Key themes:** Tribal/Kindred, Blood tokens, Counters (+1/+1), Aristocrats overlap
**Why it works:** Three true lords/payoffs (Bloodline Keeper's flip anthem, Captivating Vampire, Edgar's eminence tokens) sit on a deep curve of aggressive Vampire bodies. The Blood-token subtheme ([Voldaren Epicure](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Voldaren+Epicure"), [Gluttonous Guest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gluttonous+Guest"), [Restless Bloodseeker // Bloodsoaked Reveler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Restless+Bloodseeker")) doubles as madness fuel, bridging directly into the Madness archetype below.

**Keystones:** [Falkenrath Gorger](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Falkenrath+Gorger"), [Bloodhall Priest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodhall+Priest"), [Asylum Visitor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Asylum+Visitor")

**Key themes:** Madness, discard outlets, hellbent, Blood tokens, Wheels
**Why it works:** Ten madness cards meet a dense outlet web: Blood tokens ("{1}, {T}, Discard a card, Sacrifice this token: Draw a card"), [Noose Constrictor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Noose+Constrictor") and [Olivia's Dragoon](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Olivia's+Dragoon") (free repeatable discard), [Reckless Scholar](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Reckless+Scholar"), plus cross-tag inclusions justified by discard costs in oracle text: [Collective Brutality](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Collective+Brutality") ("Escalate—Discard a card"), [Collective Defiance](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Collective+Defiance") and [Reforge the Soul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Reforge+the+Soul") (mass-discard wheels that turn a hand of madness cards into free casts). Hellbent payoffs ([Bloodhall Priest](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bloodhall+Priest"), [Lupine Prototype](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Lupine+Prototype"), [Bedlam Reveler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bedlam+Reveler")) reward emptying your hand. Overlaps Vampires heavily — the two are frequently one BR deck.

Recursive auras (Gryff's Boon returns from the graveyard; disturb auras exile instead of dying) mitigate the classic 2-for-1 risk, and the double-strike pieces (Twinblade Invocation, Blood Mist, Uncaged Fury) turn one big threat lethal.

### Reanimator — Supported

## Build-Around

### Meld — Build-Around
**Colors:** W / R / B (three independent pairs)  **Macro lean:** Midrange
**Keystones:** [Gisela, the Broken Blade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Gisela,+the+Broken+Blade") + [Bruna, the Fading Light](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Bruna,+the+Fading+Light") → [Brisela, Voice of Nightmares](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Brisela,+Voice+of+Nightmares"); [Hanweir Garrison](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hanweir+Garrison") + [Hanweir Battlements](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hanweir+Battlements") → [Hanweir, the Writhing Township](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Hanweir,+the+Writhing+Township"); [Graf Rats](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Graf+Rats") + [Midnight Scavengers](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Midnight+Scavengers") → [Chittering Host](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Chittering+Host")
**Core cards:** all nine pieces above
**Key themes:** Meld, two-card payoffs
**Why it works:** All three meld pairs (and their melded faces) are in the cube as separate entries. Each half is independently playable — Gisela is a premium 4-drop, Hanweir Garrison is a Humans/Tokens staple, Midnight Scavengers returns a small creature — so the meld is upside, not a cost. Bruna can even reanimate Gisela to assemble Brisela from the graveyard (her cast trigger returns "target Angel or Human creature card"). A drafter who opens one half should speculate on the other; nothing else in the cube rewards it, hence Build-Around.

### Exactly Thirteen (Tree of Perdition combo) — Build-Around
**Colors:** B (G splash)  **Macro lean:** Combo/Control
**Keystones:** [Tree of Perdition](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Tree+of+Perdition"), [Triskaidekaphobia](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Triskaidekaphobia")
**Core cards:** [Cryptolith Fragment // Aurora of Emrakul](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Cryptolith+Fragment")
**Key themes:** Alternate win, life-total manipulation, Defender bodies
**Why it works:** Tree of Perdition's "{T}: Exchange target opponent's life total with this creature's toughness" sets an opponent to exactly 13; Triskaidekaphobia's upkeep trigger then wins outright ("Each player with exactly 13 life loses the game"). Cryptolith Fragment chips all life totals toward 13 while fixing mana. A true two-card build-around — a drafter who sees both late should take the free win. (Note: six creatures in the cube share the defender keyword, but nothing counts defenders, so "Defenders" was evaluated and cut as a non-archetype; Tree's real home is this combo plus stalling walls.)

## Guided Coverage — rare/mythic accounting

**85 of 97 rares/mythics placed** in at least one archetype above. The remaining **12 could not be placed**, honestly:

- **10 rare dual lands** — [Deathcap Glade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Deathcap+Glade"), [Deserted Beach](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Deserted+Beach"), [Dreamroot Cascade](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Dreamroot+Cascade"), [Haunted Ridge](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Haunted+Ridge"), [Overgrown Farmland](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Overgrown+Farmland"), [Rockfall Vale](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Rockfall+Vale"), [Shattered Sanctum](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shattered+Sanctum"), [Shipwreck Marsh](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Shipwreck+Marsh"), [Stormcarved Coast](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Stormcarved+Coast"), [Sundown Pass](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Sundown+Pass"): pure mana fixing ("{T}: Add {X} or {Y}"). They support every two-color archetype equally and belong to none — forcing them into one would be fabrication.
- **[Maelstrom Pulse](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Maelstrom+Pulse")** — "Destroy target nonland permanent": archetype-agnostic removal. No archetype in this cube uses its effect specifically.
- **[Vanquish the Horde](https://scryfall.com/search?q=!"Vanquish+the+Horde")** — a generic board wipe; its cost reduction counts *all* creatures including yours, so even token decks can't exploit it. Control staple, not an archetype card.

Notable rare/mythic homes that required cross-tag work: Chandra, Dressed to Kill / Jace, Unraveler of Secrets / Temporal Mastery → Spellslinger; Sigarda → Voltron; Unnatural Growth + Tamiyo's Journal → Tokens; Mass Hysteria → Reanimator; Heartless Summoning + Cryptolith Rite → Emerge; Tamiyo, Field Researcher → Spirits; Zealous Conscripts → Aristocrats; Helvault → Blink (fringe, with caveat); Hanweir Battlements + Brisela → Meld; Altered Ego + Metallic Mimic + Hopeful Initiate → Counters.
