UR Spellslinger Tempo chains cheap cantrips and flashback spells into an escalating board of payoffs. Delver of Secrets threatens from turn 1 while Thing in the Ice clears blockers and swings for 7. Thermo-Alchemist converts every spell into face damage, untapping for repeated pings. Bedlam Reveler refills the hand mid-game, and Burning Vengeance turns every flashback into a free Shock. Chandra, Dressed to Kill provides mana, reach, and card advantage for longer games.

Synergy Interactions
--------------------

The deck generates 2-3 spell casts per turn cycle by leveraging flashback. Faithless Looting casts twice (once from hand, once from GY), removing 2 ice counters from Thing in the Ice, untapping Thermo-Alchemist twice, dealing 2 damage from Burning Vengeance, and fueling Bedlam Reveler's discount by 4 cards in the graveyard. Galvanic Iteration can double any of these -- copying a Fiery Temper for 6 face damage, or a Rise from the Tides in post-board games for double the zombie tokens.

Flashback Density: 5 flashback cards in the mainboard (Faithless Looting, Think Twice, Silent Departure, Galvanic Iteration) plus Burning Vengeance as the payoff. Each flashback cast deals 2 to any target. In an average game with 3 flashback casts, Burning Vengeance adds 6 reach -- often the difference between stabilizing and dying.

Thing in the Ice Tension: Flipping Thing bounces your own non-Horror creatures (Thermo-Alchemist, Delver). This is by design -- you control when the last counter comes off. Flip on the opponent's end step after they commit to the board; recast Delver for U and Thermo-Alchemist for 1R on your next turn to immediately resume the spell chain. The 7/8 Awoken Horror attacks unopposed while the opponent rebuilds.

Mana Base: 16 lands with a 68% red / 32% blue pip split. Five Islands provide 7 blue sources (with 2 Molten Tributary), and nine Mountains provide 11 red sources. The R-heavy pip demand matches the R-heavy land count. Molten Tributary enters tapped, so it's sequenced on turns where the mana isn't needed immediately (turn 3 after a turn 2 Thing).

Sideboard Guide
---------------

Against aggro: +Savage Alliance, +Abrade, +Voldaren Epicure, +Borrowed Hostility; -Wandering Mind, -Ancestral Anger, -1 Think Twice, -1 Faithless Looting.

Against control: +Docent of Perfection, +Rise from the Tides, +Mystic Retrieval, +Forbidden Alchemy; -Silent Departure x2, -Lightning Axe, -Imprisoned in the Moon.

Against combo: +Summary Dismissal, +Abrade; -Savage Alliance, -Imprisoned in the Moon.

Cards Considered but Excluded
-----------------------------

Rares/mythics cut due to 5-card limit:
- Temporal Mastery (M) -- extra turn at miracle cost, but relies on topdeck luck and doesn't trigger any payoffs before the extra turn resolves. Too high variance for competitive.
- Mirrorwing Dragon (M) -- powerful Spellslinger engine, but at 5 CMC competes with Docent of Perfection and fills a similar slot. Docent generates wider value.
- Jace, Unraveler of Secrets (M) -- card draw + bounce + wincon, but Chandra's mana generation and proactive damage align better with the tempo plan.
- Hullbreaker Horror (R) -- 7 CMC flash flier that bounces permanents, but both rare slots and curve space were needed elsewhere.
- Collective Defiance (R) -- flexible escalate spell, but Galvanic Iteration at the same cost is better in this shell.

Strong uncommons considered but a tier below:
- Aberrant Researcher // Perfected Form (U) -- 4 CMC spellslinger flyer, but Wandering Mind at 3 CMC provides immediate value rather than waiting to flip.
- Furyblade Vampire (U) -- trample + grows with discard, but needs tribal/vampire support to reach its ceiling.
- Covetous Castaway // Ghostly Castigator (U) -- flashback enabler with removal on flip, but 5 CMC total is clunky.

Sideboard-consideration cards:
- Epitaph Golem -- graveyard protection, but 5 CMC deploy + 2 to activate is too slow against Relic effects.
- Geistlight Snare -- efficient counterspell only if you control Spirits or Enchantments (deck has none).
- Essence Flux -- blink protection, but resets Thing's ice counters, working against the primary win condition.
- Compelling Deterrence -- bounce at 2 CMC with a dead Zombie clause. Silent Departure at 1 CMC with flashback is better here.
