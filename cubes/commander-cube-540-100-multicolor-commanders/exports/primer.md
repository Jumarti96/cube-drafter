# Commander Cube (540 + 100 Multicolor Commanders) — Primer

## 1. Overview

The Commander Cube is a 700-card curated draft environment built around the Commander format. With 540 mainboard cards and 100 dedicated multicolor commanders, it delivers what it promises: a draft experience where every drafter can find a compelling commander and build around it.

The power level is **high-powered unpowered** — 70% of cards are rare or mythic, and the cube includes game-warping effects like Doubling Season, Elesh Norn, and Wheel of Fortune. There are no fast-mana rocks (Mana Crypt, Sol Ring, Moxen), so early turns focus on ramp and setup rather than explosive starts.

The central design philosophy is **synergy density over color balance**. Every color pair has overlapping mechanical themes — Tokens, Graveyard, Aristocrats, Counters — which means drafters aren't locked into a single pre-defined lane. The 100 multicolor commanders (including tri-color options) provide clear direction without forcing anyone into a single strategy.

## 2. The Archetypes

Each color pair and notable trio anchors one or more synergy clusters. Below are the major viable archetypes, with their supporting card counts (threshold: 35 Engine/Outlet + Enabler/Fodder cards for a fully supported archetype).

### Tokens (137 payload, 79 support) — fully viable
The cube's most pervasive theme. Token producers exist in every color, with white, green, and red as the primary engines. Key cards:

- **Mondrak, Glory Dominus** (W, Mythic): "If one or more tokens would be created under your control, twice that many of those tokens are created instead."
- **Chatterfang, Squirrel General** (BG, Mythic): "If one or more tokens would be created under your control, those tokens plus that many 1/1 green Squirrel creature tokens are created instead."
- **Doubling Season** (G, Rare): "If an effect would create one or more tokens under your control, it creates twice that many of those tokens instead."

Tokens feeds into Sacrifice (fodder), Counters (+1/+1) (wide boards for proliferate), and Tribal/Kindred (typal tokens).

### Aristocrats / Sacrifice (55 payload, 62 support) — fully viable
Sacrifice outlets and death triggers are concentrated in black, with supporting engines in red and white. This is the cube's primary grindy engine.

- **Massacre Wurm** (B, Mythic): "When this creature enters, creatures your opponents control get -2/-2 until end of turn. Whenever a creature an opponent controls dies, that player loses 2 life."
- **Elenda, the Dusk Rose** (BW, Mythic): "Lifelink. Whenever another creature dies, put a +1/+1 counter on Elenda. When Elenda dies, create X 1/1 white Vampire creature tokens with lifelink, where X is Elenda's power."
- **Teysa Karlov** (BW, Rare): "If a creature dying causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time. Creature tokens you control have vigilance and lifelink."

### Graveyard (58 payload, 58 support) — fully viable
Graveyard recursion and casting-from-graveyard effects span all colors, with black and blue providing the deepest engines. This cluster overlaps heavily with Aristocrats and Self-Mill.

- **Gix, Yawgmoth Praetor** (B, Mythic): "Whenever a creature deals combat damage to one of your opponents, its controller may pay 1 life. If they do, they draw a card. {4}{B}{B}{B}, Discard X cards: Exile the top X cards of target opponent's library. You may play lands and cast spells from among cards exiled with Gix..."
- **Feldon of the Third Path** (R, Mythic): "{2}{R}, {T}: Create a token that's a copy of target creature card in your graveyard, except it's an artifact in addition to its other types. It gains haste. Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step."
- **Victimize** (B, Uncommon): "Choose two target creature cards in your graveyard. Sacrifice a creature. If you do, return the chosen cards to the battlefield tapped."

### Tribal / Kindred (72 payload, 50 support) — fully viable
Typal synergies run deep, with Zombies, Goblins, and Elves as the most supported tribes. Tribal-Kindred cards often double as Token producers, creating density.

- The tribal cluster is spread across all five colors, with black (Zombies) and red (Goblins) holding the most concentrated card count. Look for signpost commanders like Sidisi, Brood Tyrant (Sultai Zombies) and Siege-Gang Commander (Goblin tokens).

### Artifacts (43 payload, 52 support) — fully viable
The strongest colorless-supported archetype. With 72 colorless cards in the cube, artifact synergies function as a fallback for any drafter whose primary lane closes.

- **Academy Manufactor** (Colorless, Rare): "If you would create a Clue, Food, or Treasure token, instead create one of each."
- **Helm of the Host** (Colorless, Rare): "At the beginning of combat on your turn, create a token that's a copy of equipped creature, except the token isn't legendary. That token gains haste. Equip {5}"

### Counters (+1/+1) (53 payload, 38 support) — viable
Just over the support threshold. Green anchors most of the counter placement, with white providing the widest counter distribution effects.

- **Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider** (G, Mythic): "Trample, haste. If you would put one or more counters on a permanent or player, put twice that many of each of those kinds of counters on that permanent or player instead."
- **Kalonian Hydra** (G, Mythic): "Trample. This creature enters with four +1/+1 counters on it. Whenever this creature attacks, double the number of +1/+1 counters on each creature you control."
- **Cathars' Crusade** (W, Rare): "Whenever a creature you control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on each creature you control."

### Spellslinger (45 payload, 30 support) — viable (borderline)
Just under the support threshold but well-represented in UR and R. Powerful if you can secure the key engine cards early.

- **Archmage Emeritus** (U, Rare): "Magecraft — Whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery spell, draw a card."
- **Arcane Bombardment** (R, Mythic): "Whenever you cast your first instant or sorcery spell each turn, exile an instant or sorcery card at random from your graveyard. Then copy each card exiled with this enchantment."

### Blink/ETB (24 payload, 35 support) — partial
Right at the support threshold. White and blue provide most of the blink effects and ETB targets. This is a rewarding build-around if the lane is open.

- **Preston, the Vanisher** (W, Rare): "Whenever another nontoken creature you control enters, if it wasn't cast, create a token that's a copy of that creature, except it's a 0/1 white Illusion."
- **Sun Titan** (W, Mythic): "Vigilance. Whenever this creature enters or attacks, you may return target permanent card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to the battlefield."

### Emerging Archetypes

Several clusters have payoffs but limited support, making them high-risk, high-reward build-arounds:

- **Reanimator** (24 payoff, 14 support) — dependent on entomb effects and reanimation spells. Pairs naturally with Graveyard.
- **Lifegain** (35 payoff, 18 support) — more payoffs than ways to gain life consistently. Look for soul-sister effects in white.
- **Voltron/Equipment** (18 payoff, 18 support) — spread across colors. Swords of X and Y are in the cube and worth building around.
- **Enchantress** (17 payoff, 13 support) — available but thin. A constellation-based B/G or W/G build can work.
- **Wheels** (8 payoff, 8 support) — Nekusar the Mindrazer is a strong signpost. Enough wheel effects exist to build around.
- **Storm** (6 payoff, 6 support) — exists primarily through spellslinger copy effects like Krark, the Thumbless and Thousand-Year Storm.

## 3. Color Breakdown

### White
White's primary mechanical identity is **token generation** (25 cards), **targeted removal** (16), and **blink/flicker effects** (8 cards). It anchors the Tokens, Counters (+1/+1), and Blink/ETB archetypes.

Signature cards:
- **Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite** (Mythic): "Vigilance. Other creatures you control get +2/+2. Creatures your opponents control get -2/-2." — boards become one-sided overnight.
- **Elspeth, Sun's Champion** (Mythic): "+1: Create three 1/1 white Soldier creature tokens. -3: Destroy all creatures with power 4 or greater."
- **Valkyrie's Call** (Mythic): "Whenever a nontoken, non-Angel creature you control dies, return that card to the battlefield under its owner's control with a +1/+1 counter on it. It has flying and is an Angel in addition to its other types."

### Blue
Blue's identity is **card draw** (35 cards) and **spell interaction** (all 11 counterspells in the cube). Blue also provides token generation through spell copying. It anchors Spellslinger and Graveyard casting.

Signature cards:
- **Finale of Revelation** (Mythic): "Draw X cards. If X is 10 or more, instead shuffle your graveyard into your library, draw X cards, untap up to five lands, and you have no maximum hand size for the rest of the game."
- **Sakashima of a Thousand Faces** (Mythic): "You may have Sakashima enter as a copy of another creature you control, except it has Sakashima's other abilities. The 'legend rule' doesn't apply to permanents you control. Partner."
- **Archmage Emeritus** (Rare): "Magecraft — Whenever you cast or copy an instant or sorcery spell, draw a card."

### Black
Black controls the sacrifice and reanimation axis — **11 sacrifice outlets**, **11 reanimation effects**, and **13 life drain cards**. It is the backbone of Aristocrats/Sacrifice, Graveyard, and Reanimator.

Signature cards:
- **Bitterblossom** (Mythic): "At the beginning of your upkeep, you lose 1 life and create a 1/1 black Faerie Rogue creature token with flying." — guaranteed sac fodder every turn.
- **Grave Titan** (Mythic): "Deathtouch. Whenever this creature enters or attacks, create two 2/2 black Zombie creature tokens."
- **Massacre Wurm** (Mythic): "When this creature enters, creatures your opponents control get -2/-2 until end of turn. Whenever a creature an opponent controls dies, that player loses 2 life."

### Red
Red is built around **direct damage** (32 cards), **token generation** (25), and **spell copying**. It anchors Tokens (goblins) and Spellslinger.

Signature cards:
- **Feldon of the Third Path** (Mythic): "{2}{R}, {T}: Create a token that's a copy of target creature card in your graveyard, except it's an artifact in addition to its other types. It gains haste. Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step."
- **Wheel of Fortune** (Mythic): "Each player discards their hand, then draws seven cards."
- **Arcane Bombardment** (Mythic): "Whenever you cast your first instant or sorcery spell each turn, exile an instant or sorcery card at random from your graveyard. Then copy each card exiled with this enchantment."

### Green
Green is the ramp and creature engine — **17 mana ramp spells**, **10 land fetch effects**, and creature-centric value engines. It anchors Lands-Matter, Counters (+1/+1), and Landfall.

Signature cards:
- **Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider** (Mythic): "Trample, haste. If you would put one or more counters on a permanent or player, put twice that many of each of those kinds of counters on that permanent or player instead."
- **Primeval Bounty** (Mythic): "Whenever you cast a creature spell, create a 3/3 green Beast creature token. Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, put three +1/+1 counters on target creature you control. Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, you gain 3 life."
- **Beast Whisperer** (Rare): "Whenever you cast a creature spell, draw a card."

## 4. The Environment

This is a **mid-to-late-game environment**. The CMC curve leans toward 3-4 for creatures, with a significant tail of 35 creatures at 7+ mana. Fast aggro is not the norm — decks build toward explosive turns enabled by ramp, token doubling, and sacrifice chains.

**Interaction density** sits at 23.1% of non-land cards: 99 targeted removal effects, 31 board wipes, and 11 counterspells. Threats will typically resolve and stick for a turn cycle, but every deck has access to removal. The 5.1% sweeper rate is notable — expect board stalls followed by resets, which rewards decks that can rebuild quickly through tokens or recursion.

**What's undervalued vs. normal environments:**
- **Card draw** is plentiful (133 cards). Players coming from retail draft will find draw density unusually high — games rarely go to topdeck mode.
- **Graveyard hate** is present (the tagger identifies 3 cards with "Graveyard Hate" mechanical function). Given that Graveyard is the second-largest cluster (102 cards), decks that ignore the graveyard will struggle.
- **Ramp** matters more than in typical cubes. With the long CMC tail and mana-hungry activated abilities, signets and mana rocks are premium picks.

**What's overvalued vs. normal:**
- **Life drain** (29 cards) is more common than in most environments, making life totals a resource that shifts constantly.
- **Token doublers** (Mondrak, Doubling Season, Chatterfang, Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives) are disproportionately powerful — the token cluster contains 137 cards, meaning every doubler will find targets.

## 5. Drafting the Cube

### Signals and open archetypes

**Most consistently open archetypes:**
1. **Spellslinger** (30 support cards) — UR spells-matter has enough support to be competitive, but it's not as deep as the Graveyard/Sacrifice axis. If you see Archmage Emeritus or Arcane Bombardment in mid-pack, the lane is likely open.
2. **Blink/ETB** (35 support) — at the threshold, meaning it's viable but not over-subscribed. Early Preston the Vanisher or Sun Titan is a strong signal.
3. **Counters (+1/+1)** (38 support) — just over threshold. Green and white are the primary colors, with Vorinclex and Kalonian Hydra as clear signpost cards.

**Most contested resources:**
- **Black** is the most fought-over color. It anchors three major clusters (Aristocrats, Graveyard, Reanimator) simultaneously, and its payoffs are among the strongest in the cube. Black will rarely be open past the first few picks in a draft.
- **Token synergy enablers** like Mondrak, Doubling Season, and Anointed Procession are premium picks even outside dedicated token decks, because tokens are so pervasive.

### Fixing requirements

Every color pair has 4 rare dual lands and a common Signet. Three-color decks are viable but require committing early to artifact mana. Prioritize signets over most medium-power cards — ramp is the primary enabler for the cube's mana-hungry spells.

The major tri-color combinations that are well-supported:
- **BUG (Sultai)** — Graveyard, Self-Mill, and Reanimator overlap naturally. Sidisi, Brood Tyrant is the signpost commander.
- **BRG (Jund)** — Aristocrats and Tokens through sacrifice. Ziatora, the Incinerator and Coram, the Undertaker anchor this trio.
- **BGW (Abzan)** — Counters and reanimation. Ghave, Guru of Spores and Karador, Ghost Chieftain head this wedge.
- **BRU (Grixis)** — Spellslinger and Wheels. Kess, Dissident Mage and Nekusar, the Mindrazer are signposts.

### Common traps to avoid

1. **Over-drafting 7+ CMC creatures.** There are 35 of them, and they're tempting. Unless your deck has at least 8-10 ramp sources and a plan to survive to turn 6+, leave most of them in the pack. One or two as finishers is fine; a hand of three uncastable bombs will lose games.

2. **Assuming mono-color is viable.** At 82-86 cards per color, a mono-color deck will struggle to find 30+ playables. The cube is designed for 2-3 color decks with commanders providing direction.

3. **Ignoring graveyard interaction.** Whether it's a Reanimate for an opponent's bomb or a Scavenging Ooze effect, every deck should have at least one way to interact with graveyards. There are 102 Graveyard-tagged cards — ignoring the zone is a losing proposition.

4. **Waiting too long on commanders.** With 100 dedicated multicolor commanders, you should identify your commander by pack 1's end. The cube rewards commitment — many cards reference "if you control your commander" or provide commander-specific benefits.

5. **Picking lands too late.** Rare duals will not wheel. Each color pair only has 4 dual lands, and in an 8-player draft, every pair will be contested by at least one player. Take fixing when you see it in picks 3-6 of each pack.

## 6. Mana and Fixing

### Land inventory

Every color pair has an identical fixing profile: **4 rare dual lands** and **1 common Signet**. The land base includes:

- **Original duals** (Tundra, Underground Sea, Badlands, Taiga, Savannah, Scrubland, Volcanic Island, Bayou, Plateau, Tropical Island) — one for each pair, all rare.
- **Shock lands** (Hallowed Fountain, Watery Grave, Blood Crypt, Stomping Ground, Temple Garden, Godless Shrine, Steam Vents, Overgrown Tomb, Sacred Foundry, Breeding Pool) — one for each pair, all rare.
- **Pain lands** (Adarkar Wastes, Shivan Reef, Llanowar Wastes, Brushland, Yavimaya Coast, Battlefield Forge, Karplusan Forest) — partial cycle, rare.
- **Temples/scry lands** (Temple of Enlightenment, Temple of Deceit, Temple of Malice, Temple of Abandon, Temple of Plenty, Temple of Silence, Temple of Epiphany, Temple of Malady, Temple of Triumph, Temple of Mystery) — one for each pair, all rare.
- **Miscellaneous duals** — Isolated Chapel (WB), Sunken Hollow (BU), Smoldering Marsh (BR).

Additionally, **Path of Ancestry** (common, taps for any color in your commander's identity) and **Exotic Orchard** (rare, taps for any color among lands opponents control) provide universal fixing.

### Signets

All ten Signets at common rarity: Azorius, Dimir, Rakdos, Gruul, Selesnya, Orzhov, Golgari, Simic, Izzet, Boros. These are premium fixing and ramp in one slot.

### Multi-color viability

**Two-color decks** are the baseline. With 4 duals and a signet per pair, any two-color combination has access to 5 dedicated fixers plus Path of Ancestry and colorless mana rocks.

**Three-color decks** are well-supported for the major trios (Sultai, Jund, Abzan, Grixis) but require more attention to fixing. Plan to grab signets and duals for both your primary pairs, and don't count on Path of Ancestry being in every pool — there's only one.

**Four and five-color decks** are possible, built around artifact mana and universal fixing. They will be slower but can leverage the cube's most powerful individual cards. Exotic Orchard and Path of Ancestry are the linchpins.

### Splash thresholds

A light splash (one off-color pip on 2-3 cards) needs 3-4 sources of that color. A committed third color needs 6+ sources. The cube's 91 cards at CMC 0 (mostly lands and mana rocks) mean fixing is available at every point in the draft — but you must prioritize it.

Signets are the most contested common-rarity fixers. In a typical draft, most will be taken by pack 1 pick 5. If you plan to play 3+ colors, take the first signet you see in your colors over a medium playable.
